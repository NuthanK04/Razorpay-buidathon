## 2. Core Drivers of Merchant Revenue

1. **Conversational Intent Mapping**: Reduces customer drop-off by instantly filtering and matching catalog specs to customer budgets.
2. **Context-Aware Protection & Accessory Pitches**: Explains the rationale for warranty and complementary hardware at the exact moment of purchase commitment.
3. **Zero-Friction Razorpay Checkout**: Keeps customer in flow with direct order initialization and instant signature verification.
